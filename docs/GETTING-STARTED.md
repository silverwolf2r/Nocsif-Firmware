# Getting Started with NocSif

---

## The basics

### Wake the screen
- **Double-tap** the screen.
- **Twist/shake your wrist** — a deliberate flick wakes it (a slow lift won't). Toggle under **System › Display › Shake wake/sleep**.
- **Press the Power button.**

### The two side buttons
Both buttons are on the right edge.

| Button | Tap | Hold | Double-tap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Power (PWR)** | Wake · turn screen off · back to Home · dismiss a menu | **Power menu** (Power off / Restart) | Launch a shortcut you set *(optional)* |
| **Function (FN)** | **Back** one screen | — (reserved) | Launch a shortcut app *(default: WiFi)* |

Set what the double-taps launch under **System › Buttons**.

### To move between screens
- **Swipe up** from the watchface to get to  **Home**.
- **Swipe right** from Home → to get back to the watchface.
- **Swipe down** from the top to access the **Control Center** (brightness, volume, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, flashlight, Do Not Disturb, Airplane).
- On the watchface, **tap a planet** to jump straight into that category.

## More detail (if you get stuck)

### Rearrange Home & the watchface
Home and the watchface show **planets** (app shortcuts) you can fully rearrange.

1. **Hold** down on the screen for about half a second to enter **edit mode**.
2. Tap the **＋** in the center to **add** an app — walk the folder tree and tap the one you want.
3. **Drag a planet to the trash** to remove it, or drag it to **reorder**.
4. **Tap a planet** to give it a **custom icon** from the celestial icon set.
5. **Some ring layouts need symmetry** — e.g. dual-ring needs an even number of planets.

### Change the look (Home & watchface layout)
Under **System › Display**:
- **Home layout** / **Watchface layout** — ring, dual dials, or bottom arc.
- **Home spin** / **Watchface spin** — let the planets auto-rotate.
- **Time / Date / Weather chip / Sun & moon line** — turn each watchface readout on or off.
- **Reduced motion** — collapse animations (nicer on battery).

### Theme, wallpaper & fonts
Under **System › Theme / Wallpaper / Font**:
- **Accent** — pick your color from the wheel (or a preset). It recolors the whole UI live.
- **Type scale** — tap to cycle text size: Compact → Default → Large → X-Large → XXL.
- **Wallpaper** — choose **Orrery**, **Engraved Sun**, or **Grimoire**; each keeps its own star color, layers (rings/stars/dots), and comets.

### Connect Wi-Fi
**Cyber › WiFi › Join Networks** → tap your network → type the password on-screen → **Join**. It's saved, so the watch reconnects on its own next time. (Quick toggle: the Wi-Fi tile in Control Center.)

### Connect your phone (Bluetooth)
**Cyber › Bluetooth LE › Connect Phone** → pair when your phone prompts. Once bonded, the watch can mirror phone notifications and act as a media remote.

### Set up Weather
**Life › Weather** needs Wi-Fi and a location. It **auto-follows your last GPS fix**, so grab a GPS fix once (see below), then open Weather and **Refresh**. Tap to toggle °C/°F. Your local temperature then shows on the watchface chip.

### Get a GPS fix
**Cyber › Location / GNSS › Live Fix**, then **go outside with a clear view of the sky**. The **first fix (cold start) can take a few minutes — up to ~5** — so give it time. After that it locks on much faster.

## What's on the watch

Apps are grouped into three categories: **Cyber**, **Life**, and **System**.

> ⚠️ **Use responsibly.** The Cyber tools are for learning and for testing networks and devices **you own or are explicitly authorized to test**. You are responsible for how you use them.

### Cyber — radios, protocols & discovery

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| **WiFi** | Join networks; scan live networks, clients & probe requests; monitor/record 2.4 GHz traffic; capture handshakes/PMKID; flag anomalies; run a Software AP, Captive Portal or Beacon TX. |
| **Bluetooth LE** | Connect your phone (notifications + media); scan devices; find nearby item trackers; detect drones (Remote ID); explore a device's GATT services; capture adverts; broadcast your own advertisement/beacon. |
| **NFC** | 13.56 MHz NFC tools. *(Note: NFC transmit is hardware-limited on some units — see FAQ.)* |
| **USB Gadget** | Turn the watch into a USB drive (share the microSD to a PC) or a USB keyboard that runs DuckyScript macros. |
| **Audio** | Play tones and cart-lock tones through the speaker. |
| **Sub-GHz / LoRa** | Peer-to-peer LoRa messaging; scan channel activity; run a full band survey. |
| **Location / GNSS** | Live GPS fix, GPX track logging, set the clock from GPS, and Wardrive (geotagged Wi-Fi survey). |
| **Signal Hunt** | Pin a Wi-Fi/BLE/LoRa signal and hunt it by strength — "getting warmer." |

### Life — day-to-day

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Alerts** | One feed of the watch's notifications; swipe to dismiss. |
| **Do Not Disturb** | Silence chimes/alerts. |
| **Timers & Alarms** | Stopwatch, countdown timer, world clock, and alarms. |
| **Voice Memos** | Record and play back short voice notes. |
| **Weather** | Current conditions + 3-day forecast (see setup above). |
| **Notes** | Jot and store quick text notes on the card. |
| **Flashlight** | Full-brightness white screen; tap to exit. |

### System — settings & tools

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Files** | Browse, view and delete files on the microSD. |
| **GeoFence** | Location-based automations. |
| **Watch Name** | Set the name the watch shows on networks. |
| **Display** | Layouts, motion, wake, and watchface readouts. |
| **Power** | Screen timeout, sleep behavior, and battery saver. |
| **Buttons** | Bind the FN and PWR double-tap shortcuts. |
| **Theme / Wallpaper / Font** | Accent color, text size, wallpaper. |
| **PIN Lock** | Require a passcode to leave the watchface. |
| **Connectivity** | Live status of Wi-Fi / phone / Sub-GHz / GPS. |
| **Companion** | A phone/laptop web remote for the watch over Wi-Fi. |

| **About** · **Diagnostics** | Device info; logs and crash records. |
| **Microphone** · **Sound** | Mic level meter; volume, mute and cue settings. |

## Tips & tricks

- **Teach it your home Wi-Fi to save battery.** NocSif learns *where* your saved networks live: it ties a network to the GPS location where you connected. Once learned, it **parks Wi-Fi to save power when you're away** and **wakes it automatically when you come back**. To teach it home: connect to your home Wi-Fi once **with a GPS fix** (stand near a window or step outside briefly so it can stamp the location). After that, home reconnects on its own and Wi-Fi stays parked everywhere else.
- **First GPS fix is the slow one.** Cold starts take a few minutes outdoors. Later fixes are quick — and once weather has a location, it keeps refreshing on its own.
- **Battery Saver in one tap.** **System › Power › Battery Saver** bundles CPU down-clock + short timeout + dimmer screen + reduced motion. Turn on **auto** and it kicks in by itself below 20% while unplugged.
- **Movie mode** (Control Center) keeps the screen dim and quiet and stops shake-wake — good for a dark room.
- **Set a shortcut.** Bind the **FN double-tap** (default WiFi) and a **PWR double-tap** to your most-used apps under **System › Buttons**.
- **Lock it.** Set a passcode in **System › PIN Lock** to require it before leaving the watchface.
- **Control it from your phone.** **System › Companion** hosts a web remote — open the shown address in your phone's browser on the same network.

## FAQ

**The screen went black — is it off?**
It's asleep to save power. Double-tap it, twist your wrist, or press Power. Adjust the timeout under **System › Power › Screen timeout**.

**My GPS won't get a fix.**
GPS needs open sky — it won't fix well indoors. Go outside and wait; the first cold fix can take up to ~5 minutes sometimes more if god hates you like he hated me.

**Weather is blank or won't update.**
Weather needs **Wi-Fi** and a **location**. Connect to Wi-Fi and get a location fix, then open **Life › Weather** and tap **Refresh**.

**Wi-Fi won't reconnect / keeps turning off.**
That's the battery-saving Governor parking idle Wi-Fi. It wakes on its own at a known location, or when you tap the Wi-Fi tile in Control Center. See the tip above about teaching it your home network. You can also turn this off if you'd like.

**How do I share files with my computer?**
**Cyber › USB Gadget** exposes the microSD as a USB drive when plugged into a PC.

**How do I update the firmware?**
**System › Update (OTA)** checks for and installs updates, with automatic rollback if an update doesn't boot cleanly.

**NFC / haptic buzz doesn't work.**
This is broken on my watch, so I haven't been able to code for it yet. Once LilyGo restocks the watch I'll buy one and add it.
