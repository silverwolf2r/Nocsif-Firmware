What changed vs. v1

Bigger compass, needle = strength

The compass is the whole screen now. Instead of a warmer/colder line or a filled lobe, the needle brightens smoothly as the signal climbs — strength lives in the one element you're already reading for direction.

Accent = rose, needle & found dot

The compass rose, the resolved needle, and the calibration dot sitting over the emitter all take the system accent colour (violet shown).

Grey = a heading you've covered

The twelve dots fill grey as you rotate through each 30° sector — a visible "you've swept enough" so the bearing is trustworthy.

Spin is the instruction

Until you've swept, a pulsing spin prompt sits dead-centre. It clears the moment the sweep completes.

Try it. Grab the dial and drag to spin (or wait for the auto-sweep). Fill the dots, watch the needle resolve to the emitter and brighten as the sim "walks in". Reset sweep re-seeds; the audio toggle is the eyes-free mute.
